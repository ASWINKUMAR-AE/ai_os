# 🤖 AI Desktop Assistant
> **Your Intelligent Workspace Companion**

---

## 🌟 What is this Assistant?
The **AI Desktop Assistant** is a premium, state-of-the-art software suite designed to unify Artificial Intelligence with your local desktop environment. Built on the robust **Electron.js** framework, it provides a seamless bridge between cutting-edge LLMs (via OpenRouter) and your daily computing tasks. It is more than just a chatbot; it's an autonomous command center for your productivity.

## 🚀 What does it do?
This assistant transforms how you interact with your computer. Instead of navigating through menus and typing manually, you can simply **tell or type** what you need, and the assistant executes it. It handles everything from complex research and creative writing to mundane system tasks like opening applications, managing folders, and controlling your PC's state—all while maintaining a sleek, modern, and high-performance interface.

---

## ⚙️ Core Modules
The application is powered by several intelligently integrated engines:

*   **🎙️ Voice Intelligence**: High-accuracy speech-to-text (STT) and human-like text-to-speech (TTS) for hands-free interaction.
*   **🧠 Unified AI Reasoning**: Powered by **OpenRouter**, utilizing models like *Gemini 2.0 Flash*, *Claude 3.5 Sonnet*, and *GPT-4o* with full context of your OS environment.
*   **🛠️ System Automation**: A direct interface with your Operating System for application launching, file management, and system state control.
*   **⚡ Workflow Engine**: A sequence-based automation system for "one-click" execution of complex multi-step routines (e.g., "Start Work Mode").

---

## ✨ Key Features

### 🔗 Direct Mode (App Linking)
*   **Live Injection**: Link the AI Assistant to any active application (Notepad, Word, VS Code).
*   **Automatic Typing**: The AI can write directly into your target app as if you were typing yourself.
*   **Smart Selection**: The app remembers your last used windows for seamless content transfer.

### 💬 Intelligent AI Chat
*   **Streaming Responses**: Real-time feedback with a natural conversational flow.
*   **Environment Awareness**: The AI knows your local time, OS platform, and what apps you have open.
*   **Syntax Mastery**: Full Markdown support with beautiful code highlighting via Highlight.js.

### 🎙️ Advanced Voice Control
*   **Continuous Listening**: A "Jarvis-like" experience where the app stays alert for your commands.
*   **Visual Feedback**: Animated real-time wave visualizers that react dynamically to your speech.

### ✍️ AI Content Writer
*   **Specialized Templates**: Optimized modes for Blog Posts, Professional Emails, Social Media, and Code.
*   **Granular Control**: Fine-tune the Tone (Professional, Casual, etc.) and Length of your generated content.

### 🤖 Task & Compound Automation
*   **Natural Language Tasks**: "Create a folder called Projects on my Desktop" or "Find all files named budget".
*   **Compound Actions**: Combine app launching and writing: *"In Notepad: write a quick summary of the current AI trends."*
*   **Fast Commands**: Use `/` for instant, high-speed system actions without AI latency.

### 🎨 Premium Aesthetics
*   **Glassmorphism UI**: A stunning dark-themed interface with translucent elements, smooth transitions, and modern typography.
*   **Responsive Layout**: Designed to feel like a native part of your workspace, whether docked or windowed.
